My love, I'm genuinely sorry for hurting you. I'm sorry for the moments when my words or actions made your heart heavy, when I made you feel anything less than loved, safe, and valued. You didn't deserve that hurt. I know saying “sorry” is easy. What matters is whether I listen, understand what hurt you, take responsibility without making excuses, and actually change. I don't want to just ask you to forget what happened. I want to learn from it and show you, through my actions, that your feelings matter to me. You matter to me so much. I want to be someone you can talk to honestly, someone who hears you instead of getting defensive, someone who takes care with your heart instead of assuming you'll always be okay. I want to share the hard parts of life with you, make the ordinary days sweeter, and build something that feels peaceful for both of us. I can't promise that life will never be difficult or that I'll never make a mistake. I can promise to keep learning, to be honest with you, to own my mistakes, and to work to make things right when I get them wrong. I want to care for us—not with big words alone, but with little things I do consistently. If there's room in your heart, I'd be grateful for a chance to show you that. You don't have to decide anything right now, and you don't owe me an immediate answer. I'll respect your feelings and your time. I'm sorry, my love. I love you, and I want to love you better. ♡
